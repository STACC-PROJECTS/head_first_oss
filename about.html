About Chaayakudi

Chaayakudi started as a shared spreadsheet between three hostel roommates who got tired of doing chaya-money math by hand. It grew into a small site for mapping chaya spots across CUSAT and splitting the bill fairly, every single time.

How Bill Splitting Works

When your group grabs chaya together, Chaayakudi seperates the total bill equally so nobody's stuck doing math on a napkin. Just enter the total and how many people were there.

Your Chayakudi Wrapped

Once your group hits enough chaya visits in a semester, you unlock a Wrapped card: total chaya count, your group's most-visited spot, who spent the most, and your longest chaya streak. See script/chayakudi-utils.py for how it's calculated.
